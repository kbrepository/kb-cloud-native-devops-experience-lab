# Engineering Principles

## Principle 1

Understand the problem before learning the technology.

---

## Principle 2

Every technology exists because of a real engineering problem.

---

## Principle 3

Never memorize definitions.

Understand decisions.

---

## Principle 4

Every implementation should answer

"Why?"

not only

"How?"

---

## Principle 5

If something breaks,

don't immediately fix it.

Observe.

Think.

Form a hypothesis.

Then investigate.

---

## Principle 6

Failures teach more than successful deployments.

---

## Principle 7

Every failure becomes documentation.

Every document becomes interview preparation.

---

## Principle 8

Build systems that you can explain.

Not systems you copied.

---

## Principle 9

Production thinking begins long before production deployment.

---

## Principle 10

Confidence comes from understanding,

not from memorization.
